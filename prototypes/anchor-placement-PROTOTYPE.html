How does a player place Anchors?

The mod runs only on your computer, so it can't add its own item. Something you already have has to set the two Anchors of a Line. This page compares three ways to do that. All three work in the same small world, seen from the side. Move your mouse over the world to choose the block you're looking at, then press the keys and clicks below. Watch the red lines in the log. They show when a click still reaches the server, which breaks or places a real block in a real world.
